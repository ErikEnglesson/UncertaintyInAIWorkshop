# Workshop on Uncertainty Estimation in AI for the Accelerate Science Programme for Scientific Discovery
Neural networks have had success in science and engineering predictive tasks such as computer vision, biology, medicine, and weather. To safely use (make decisions based on) the predictions of neural networks, the predictions need to have reliable uncertainty estimates. Yet, despite their importance, reliable predictive uncertainty estimates are often overlooked in practice. This might be due to the fact that producing such estimates is difficult. By providing an honest overview of modern uncertainty estimation methods, the participants can decide whether to adopt them in their own research.

The aim of the workshop is two-fold. First, to introduce what uncertainty is, how to estimate it, and how to evaluate it in the context of neural networks. Second, to give participants hands-on experience by implementing and evaluating uncertainty methods, such as Deep Ensembles, Variational Inference, and Laplace approximations.
